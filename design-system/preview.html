05 / Design rules

  • Keep task context visible while reading a selected item.
  • Use padding and typography before adding another card or border.
  • Reserve the darkest fill for the most useful action.
  • Keep metadata calm, readable and aligned in a dedicated inspector.
  • Preserve selection and scroll when data updates; mark stale data explicitly.
