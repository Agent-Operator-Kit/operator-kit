## Assignment

Develop one clearly differentiated response to the shared brief. Treat this
proposal as a sibling of exactly two alternatives, not as the selected answer.

tradeoffs.

## Required Evidence

Write proposal evidence only inside this proposal directory. At minimum,
create `README.md` with:

- the direction and the problem it emphasizes;
- a concrete screen or flow outline;
- design-system and component implications;
- assumptions, risks, and rejected choices;
- validation questions for the human reviewer; and
- an inventory of any mockups, images, prototypes, or other evidence.

Optional HTML, images, exports, and supporting notes belong beside the README.
Do not modify production UI, shared design-system files, graph state, feedback
state, bindings, authority material, or artifacts belonging to another
proposal.
